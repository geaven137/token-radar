How implied value is computed

One function scores every row, from the snapshot's figures alone. No protocol name is special-cased and no row is hand-picked.

implied = round( tvl_usd × sector × (1 + clamp(change_7d, −50, 100) ÷ 100 × 0.40) × (0.70 + 0.30 × min(age_days, 730) ÷ 730) )

Sector is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the snapshot's live protocols in the same category — a lending protocol is valued like live lending protocols, a bridge like live bridges — recomputed from the snapshot on every run. Categories with fewer than three live protocols take the global median instead. Growth rewards seven-day momentum without letting one wild week dominate. Maturity rewards a long record of running with no token; an unlisted age takes 0.85.

How farm plans are built

Each plan is derived from the protocol's own record: the category decides the core action, TVL sets the capital ($50–$250), age sets the hold (30–60 days), and the first chain sets the gas tier. Every step states what it costs and how long it takes.

Data: a sealed DefiLlama snapshot, fetched once. “No token” means the record carries none — a protocol may still have a token its record does not show. Not investment advice.
