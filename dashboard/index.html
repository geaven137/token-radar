How implied value is computed

One fitted function scores every row, from the snapshot's figures alone. A ridge regression on the snapshot's live protocols predicts log(market cap / TVL) from size, age and category — the size curve is fitted, not assumed: large protocols trade at far lower multiples than small ones. Token odds come from the share of protocols that already record a token at each age, shrunk toward the global share. The range is the leave-one-out residual quartiles, so it shows how wide the fit's own misses are. No protocol name is special-cased and no row is hand-picked.

implied = round( TVL × clamp(exp(prediction), 0.002, 5) × token odds )

The fit, held out

Leave-one-out refits the model 118 times, each time holding one live protocol out; walk-forward fits on the oldest 70% by listing date and tests on the newest 30%. “Flat” is the intercept-only model — one median multiple for every protocol. The fit beats flat on both tests, and the range covers about half of the held-out protocols, as a P25–P75 range should.

Which terms survived

Each candidate term was tested by dropping it: a term stays only if dropping it worsens the leave-one-out median or mean by more than 0.5%. The squared size term and seven-day growth did not survive — growth's fitted sign was negative, a mechanical artifact of TVL spikes, so the model refuses to reward a hot week.

Try the farm economics

Verdicts are priced on the P25 value — FARM means worth it even on the downside. Move the airdrop share or the farmer count and the verdicts re-price in the page, from the same figures. The ranking never changes: implied value does not depend on these two assumptions.

How farm plans and verdicts are built

Each plan is derived from the protocol's own record: the category decides the core action, TVL sets the capital ($50–$250), age sets the hold (30–60 days), and the first chain sets the gas tier. Every step states what it costs and how long it takes. The verdict prices the P25 value: a 5% airdrop pool split across an estimated farmer count (TVL ÷ $10,000, clamped to 1,000–100,000 — a rough proxy, stated here), minus gas. FARM needs a net of at least $25 and $5 an hour; MARGINAL needs a net of at least $0; otherwise SKIP.

Data: a sealed DefiLlama snapshot, fetched once. “No token” means the record carries none — a protocol may still have a token its record does not show. Not investment advice.
